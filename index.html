About Me

I'm a final-year Artificial Intelligence student at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Assiut University. My work lives at the intersection of autonomous agent design and production-grade machine learning systems.

I specialize in building multi-step LLM reasoning pipelines, autonomous agent workflows, and robust ML systems — from data ingestion through deployment. I believe the future of software is agentic: systems that reason, plan, adapt, and act on behalf of their users.

When I'm not orchestrating AI agents, you'll find me exploring cutting-edge research papers, contributing to open-source projects, or mentoring fellow students in our university's AI community.
